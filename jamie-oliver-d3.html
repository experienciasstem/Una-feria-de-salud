Jamie Oliver

Jamie Oliver is an English chef. He is married and has five children: Poppy Honey Rosie, Daisy Boo Pamela, Petal Blossom Rainbow, Buddy Bear Maurice and River Rocket. Jamie's wife, Jools, is a model and fashion designer. Jamie is 48 years old and his wife is 49. They live in London, UK.

Jamie is famous for promoting healthy habits. He is the author of two cookbooks: Super Food Family Classics and Everyday Super Food. He was the host of The Naked Chef (1999) and Jamie Oliver's Food Revolution (2010–11).

Jamie's Top Tips on How to Live Well Follow these tips:
🍳 Eat breakfast Start the day with a healthy breakfast. You need energy for your daily activities.
🥦 Fruit & veg Eat fruit and vegetables every day — rich in vitamins and minerals.
🌾 Wholegrain* carbs Choose wholemeal* bread, pasta and rice. Rich in fibre, vitamins and energy.
🥕 More fibre Spinach, carrots, broccoli, peas and beetroot. A high-fibre diet benefits your health.
🥚 Control protein** Fish, meat, eggs, milk and yoghurt. Control intake** for a balanced diet.
🌿 Choose organic Whenever you can***, buy organic food — good for you and the planet.
🥛 Include dairy Milk, yoghurt and cheese have lots of nutrients. Include balanced portions daily.
💧 Stay hydrated Drink 6–8 glasses of water a day. Avoid fizzy drinks high in sugar.
😴 Get sleep Sleep 7–8 hours. Avoid screens before bedtime for better rest.
🚴 Get moving Ride a bike, walk, use the stairs instead of the lift****. Stay active every day.
* wholegrain / wholemeal: integral ** intake: consumo *** whenever you can: cuando puedas **** lift: ascensor
